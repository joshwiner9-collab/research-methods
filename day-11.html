Day 11 · Review

The sweep

Every topic, one task each, about five minutes each. Then your error log, top to bottom.

One task per day

Do each on paper first, about five minutes, then open the answer. If one wobbles, reopen that day.

Day 1 · Variables. Hotel stars coded 1 to 5. Type, and which centres are legal?
Qualitative, ordinal. Mode and median; the mean assumes equal gaps the scale does not promise.
Day 2 · Tables. x = 0, 1, 2, 3 with f = 12, 18, 14, 6. How many values are at least 2?
n = 50, F(1) = 30, so 50 − 30 = 20.
Day 3 · Centre. 30 people averaged 40, and 10 people averaged 80. Overall mean?
(30·40 + 10·80) / 40 = 2,000 / 40 = 50. Not 60.
Day 4 · Spread. Mean 50, s 10. Every value is doubled and then 5 is added. New mean, s and variance?
Mean 105, s 20, variance 400. The 5 touches only the mean.
Day 5 · Position. Mean 70, s 8. Which score has z = +1.25? Shape unknown: at least what share lies within 2 s?
x = 70 + 1.25·8 = 80. Chebyshev: at least 75%.
Day 6 · Tests. n = 16, x̄ = 104, s = 8, H1: μ > 100, α = 5%. Verdict?
SE = 8/4 = 2, t = 2, df 15. One-tail 0.05 = 1.753. Reject: enough evidence that μ > 100.
Day 7 · Correlation. cov(x, y) = −12, sₓ = 4, sᵧ = 5. What is r, and where on the ladder?
r = −12/20 = −0.6: medium, negative.
Day 8 · Regression. x̄ = 4, ȳ = 20, slope 3. Intercept? Predicted y at x = 6? If r = 0.9, what is R²?
β̂₀ = 20 − 3·4 = 8. ŷ(6) = 26. R² = 0.81.
