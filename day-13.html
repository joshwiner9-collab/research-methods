Three familiar tasks

Read the table. Two samples of 14 and 14, "the means differ", α = 5%. Critical value?
df = 26, two-tail 0.05: 2.056.
Grouped median. Classes 0–10: 20, 10–20: 40, 20–40: 40 (n = 100). Median?
F = 20, 60. n/2 = 50 falls in 10–20: 10 + (50 − 20)/40 × 10 = 17.5.
From R² to r. R² = 0.36, and the line slopes down. r?
−0.6.

The trap list

Every row is a mistake the course materials call out. Print it and bring it: notes are allowed.

TrapThe right move
Digits as labelsIDs, phone numbers and shirt numbers are nominal.
Ratings 1 to 5Ordinal. Their mean is a risky shortcut.
At least xn minus F of the value one step below x.
Uneven widthsRank crowding by d = f/ℓ, never by f.
Grouped medianWalk into the class. The midpoint is the trap answer.
Grouped modeMidpoint of the highest-density class.
Combined meanWeight each group by its size.
Σ rulesΣx² is not (Σx)². Σxy is not Σx·Σy.
Divide by nAlways. Raw quartile positions use n + 1.
+a and ×bAdding never changes s. Multiplying scales s by |b| and s² by b².
SkewRight skew: more than half sit below the mean. Fifth decile = median.
zStandardize against the person's own group. Raises move salaries, not z.
CVNot for means near zero, °C or rating codes.
Bell or not68-95-99.7 needs a bell. Otherwise Chebyshev: at least 1 − 1/k².
Table headerOne direction reads one-tail; "differs" reads two-tail.
Standard errors/√n. Two samples: add the squares under one root.
dfTwo samples: n₁ + n₂ − 2, unless the question states its own rule. Not printed: nearest smaller row.
Lower tailReject only below −t*.
WordingNever "accept H0". Write "not enough evidence to reject".
Both errorsOnly a bigger sample lowers α and β together.
rr = 0 means no straight-line link. One outlier can make or break r.
R²Never negative. r = ±√R², with the slope's sign.
Residualy − ŷ, data minus model.
Printout tTests "= 0". For β = 1, compute (β̂ − 1)/SE.
ExtrapolationNo predictions outside the data. A slope is association, not cause.
